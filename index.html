Privacy

Your weather should feel personal, not invasive.

Vane is built with privacy in mind. Your Sense profile, check-ins, and saved places can sync through your private iCloud database so they follow you across Vane apps on devices signed in to your Apple Account. Vane has no ads, no cross-app tracking, and no separate Vane account.
